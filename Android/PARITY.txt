Android/iOS parity status — 2026-11-08

Implemented Android counterparts:
- Agenda, month/day/three-day/week/year calendar navigation, reminders, settings.
- Month/day arrows and horizontal month/day swipe regions.
- Shared iOS Light, Dark, Graphite, Midnight, Forest, Studio, and Custom palettes.
- Accent/custom colors and grouped settings categories.
- Half-height Quick Add with dictation handoff, event/reminder choice, editable
  dates, all-day events, and undated local reminders.
- iCloud calendar discovery, selected calendars, cached reading, nonrecurring
  event creation/editing/deletion, ETag conflict checks, scheduled/manual sync.
- Recurring event expansion including exclusions and detached exceptions.
- Optional birthday/contact dates with icons, annual year counts, gray default.
- Local reminder lists, list reordering, editing, completion and dated alerts.
- Weekly planner with scheduling for undated/overdue local reminders.
- All 11 widget types, including scrollable content, day selectors, task completion,
  undo, per-instance settings, themes, fonts, sizes, spacing, calendars, and reset.

Not yet equivalent to iOS:
- Modern iCloud Reminders sync is not supported by Apple's app-password protocol.
- Live iCloud account verification has not been performed; credentials must be
  entered by the user in the app on the test device.
- Recurring events are read-only; recurring-series/occurrence editing and advanced
  invitation/attendee features are not ported.
- Quick Add's full natural-language grammar and recurrence parsing are not ported.
- Timeline drag/resize, ICS import/share, calendar sets, per-contact selection,
  contact-date notifications, preparation/travel planning and route alerts remain.
- The Android UI and widgets have not received a pixel-by-pixel visual comparison
  against current iOS screenshots; do not describe this as an identical finished port.
- Per-widget calendar and local reminder-list subsets are implemented.
- Local task notifications are inexact; no ongoing next-event notification yet.
- No Play Store signing/distribution setup or physical Android device installation.

Verification is tracked by Gradle unit tests, Android runtime tests, lint,
and development APK builds. No real account secrets are included.
