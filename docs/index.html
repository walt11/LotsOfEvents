Get in touch

Email LotsOfEvents@jwalter.net for questions, problems, or feature ideas.

Please include your device, operating-system version, and the app version shown in Settings → Advanced → About. Describe what happened and what you expected. Please remove private event details from any screenshots you send.

Connect your calendars and reminders

Allow access when asked, then choose your calendars and reminder lists in Settings → Calendars & Reminders. LotsOfEvents uses the calendar accounts already configured on your device.

Missing an event or task?

Check that the item appears in Apple Calendar or Reminders and that its calendar or list is selected in LotsOfEvents. Pull to refresh. If access was denied, review the app’s Calendar and Reminders permissions in your device’s Settings.

Add a widget

Open your device’s widget gallery, search for LotsOfEvents, and choose a layout. Touch and hold the widget to edit its options. The app’s Settings also includes widget appearance controls.

Use Quick Add

Type what you want to add, then review the type, date, time, and destination before saving. Dictation is optional and requires microphone and speech-recognition permission.

Receive reminder notifications

Allow notifications for LotsOfEvents when prompted. You can turn its reminder alerts on or off in Settings → Alerts. To avoid duplicate alerts, turn off Apple Reminders notifications in system Settings. Existing reminder alarms are preserved. Open LotsOfEvents after editing tasks on another device to refresh upcoming alerts. Location-based alerts still use Apple Reminders. Reminders with only a date use your Default Alerts settings.

Keep control of your information

Events and tasks remain in your configured calendar and reminder accounts. Changes made in LotsOfEvents can also appear in Apple Calendar and Reminders. Read our privacy policy for details.
