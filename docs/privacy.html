Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026. LotsOfEvents is developed by John Walter. This policy explains how the app handles information and how to contact us.

Calendar and reminder information

With your permission, LotsOfEvents reads and changes events, calendars, reminders, and reminder lists through Apple’s system frameworks. This information is used to display your schedule, manage tasks, and provide widgets. It stays in your device’s configured calendar and reminder stores, which may synchronize through iCloud or another provider you have chosen. LotsOfEvents does not upload your calendar or reminder database to a server operated by the developer.

Local settings, widgets, and Apple Watch

The app saves preferences and caches the information needed for its features on your device. It shares relevant information with its widgets and extensions using Apple’s app-group storage. The companion Apple Watch app reads the watch’s own Calendar and Reminders stores with your permission. Synchronization of those stores is managed by your device and account settings.

Optional permissions and Apple services

  • Dictation: microphone audio is passed to Apple’s speech-recognition framework. Depending on your device, language, and system settings, recognition may use Apple’s servers. The developer does not receive or retain your recordings.
  • Travel: optional travel features use location information and Apple Maps services. Route calculations may use an event’s destination. Information sent to these services is governed by Apple’s privacy practices.
  • Contacts: optional contact-date features read the names, birthdays, anniversaries, and other dated fields of contacts you permit. These dates appear in the calendar, agenda, and widgets. A local app-group cache contains only the names, date fields, labels, and identifiers needed for this feature. Turning off Dates from Contacts clears that cache; changes to access are applied when the app next refreshes. Contact information is not sent to the developer. When a birth year is saved, it is used on your device to show ages and anniversary years.
  • Notifications and Live Activities: these features can show event or reminder information on your device’s screens according to your system settings.

You can decline optional permissions and change them later in system Settings. Core calendar and reminder functions require the corresponding access.

Tracking, advertising, and analytics

LotsOfEvents does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. The developer does not sell your personal information. Apple may provide diagnostics or App Store analytics according to your Apple privacy settings.

Files, links, and sharing

Calendar files you choose to import are processed to create events. Opening external links or sharing information uses the app or service you select; those services apply their own privacy policies.

Support messages

If you email support, the developer receives the address, message, and attachments you send and uses them to respond and troubleshoot. Please send only the information needed for your request. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the address below, subject to any necessary legal retention.

Your choices and retention

You can revoke permissions in system Settings and remove app preferences and caches by deleting the app. Deleting LotsOfEvents does not automatically delete events or reminders from your calendar accounts. Manage those items in LotsOfEvents, Apple Calendar, Apple Reminders, or your account provider. Changes to shared or synchronized calendars may affect other devices or participants.

Children

The app does not require a developer-operated account and is not designed to collect children’s personal information. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information directly to support.

This website

These static support pages do not contain advertising, analytics scripts, external fonts, or tracking cookies added by the developer. They are hosted by pCloud, which may process technical request information under its own privacy policy.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
